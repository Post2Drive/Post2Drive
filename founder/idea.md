# Post2Drive — two-sided freight and courier marketplace

- **What it is:** A marketplace where businesses and other load posters publish delivery/freight jobs and drivers or transport operators discover, bid on, accept and fulfil them.
- **Who it is for:** UK owner-drivers and small courier/freight operators first; a separately localised India market is also in scope. Posters include businesses, shippers and operators with overflow or return loads.
- **What it sells, at what price:** Current product model: paid access for drivers/operators in the UK and supported EUR markets, with a one-off 7-day access period and no per-job fee. Published tiers in the app README: UK Individual £9.99; fleet 2–5 £16.99; 6–15 £24.99; 16–30 £36.99; 31–55 £49.99; 56+ custom. India pricing is not specified in the current source reviewed and must be decided separately. Payment-provider integration is not verified end to end; do not accept real payments yet.
- **Where and how:** Digital marketplace; country/currency are selected at signup and are intended to remain immutable. UK is a distinct GB/GBP market; India is IN/INR; euro markets are separately localised.
- **Budget and constraints:** No validated acquisition budget, customer acquisition cost, paid conversion, retention, available jobs per active driver, or market-level unit economics were provided in the source material reviewed. Treat these as unknowns, not zero. Product is in active development and is not cleared for unrestricted public launch.
- **Known product areas:** Signup/login, job posting/search, bids, acceptance and job lifecycle, vehicle records, ratings, admin/operational dashboards and subscription-related status flows exist in the codebase; live email verification and provider-backed payments are not verified end to end.
- **Evidence note:** Product details and UK pricing were taken from the Post2Drive application README on branch `feature/country-localisation-audit`. No evidence of actual demand or revenue was supplied with this brief.

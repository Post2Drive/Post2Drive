# Brand direction — Post2Drive

## Positioning
A transparent, practical marketplace for finding and managing delivery/freight work. Trust and job relevance should lead; “more jobs” or “higher earnings” claims require evidence.

## Voice
- Clear, direct and respectful of drivers' time.
- Specific about access price, duration, job details and limitations.
- Never hype earnings or imply guaranteed work.
- Explain UK and India availability independently.

## Visual direction
Keep the existing Post2Drive identity and product design system. Use high-contrast, road-ready UI; readable type; clear job status and location; simple price/access explanations. Do not create a new logo or change product colours as part of this strategy draft.

## First five touchpoints
1. Landing page: exact supported region/vehicle type and honest job-supply status.
2. Signup: explain country/currency selection and market eligibility.
3. Job card: route, vehicle/cargo needs, pickup timing, price/rate if supplied, payment terms if known, poster status and listing freshness.
4. Access screen: price, exact 7-day duration, expiry, no automatic recurring billing, no per-job platform fee.
5. Support/reporting: clear path for suspicious listings, cancellation and disputes.

## Evidence needed
User interviews and usability sessions to establish which trust signals matter most. Any trust/verification badge must reflect a real, documented check.

# Post2Drive — founder summary

**Verdict: Incomplete — business viability is not established by the available evidence.**

Post2Drive is a two-sided freight/courier marketplace with a current UK weekly-access price hypothesis and separate market handling for India and euro markets. The core idea addresses a real operational job—matching available delivery capacity with work—but the evidence gathered so far establishes competition and product intent, not customer demand or profitability.

## Three most important findings
1. **Liquidity is the main risk.** A driver subscription only feels worth paying for when relevant jobs are consistently available and trustworthy in the driver's area and vehicle class.
2. **There are substitutes with different payment models.** UK alternatives include paid load boards and free-to-join commission models; India has established load-booking and freight networks. Post2Drive must prove why its particular offer is worth switching to.
3. **The safest next step is a narrow pilot, not broad paid acquisition.** Recruit posters first, then a matched driver cohort in one geography/segment and measure first relevant job, bids, accepted/completed work and repeat use.

## What remains unknown
No reliable cohort conversion, repeat usage, local job density, CAC, churn, revenue, contribution margin, support cost, India pricing or real buyer price answers were supplied. The CFO verdict must remain incomplete until actual numbers are entered. Simulated buyer panels are not real demand evidence.

## One thing to do this week
Conduct 15–20 interviews with active drivers and 8–10 interviews with repeat load posters in a single proposed UK launch area. Use their current tools, job frequency, route/vehicle mix and willingness to pay to choose the pilot segment. Do not commit meaningful paid marketing spend until the pilot shows repeat value.

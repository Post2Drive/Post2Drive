# Post2Drive — initial pitch (current model, not validated)

Find delivery and freight opportunities that fit your vehicle and route. Browse jobs, bid, accept work and manage the delivery workflow in one marketplace. UK access is currently designed as a one-off 7-day pass starting at £9.99 for an individual, with no per-job platform fee and no automatic recurring billing.

**Important:** This describes the intended product and pricing logic, not a promise of job volume, earnings, verified live supply or live checkout. Payment-provider integration and end-to-end launch readiness are not yet confirmed.

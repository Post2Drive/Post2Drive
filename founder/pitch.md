# Post2Drive — pilot offer hypothesis

**See relevant jobs before committing to paid access.** Join a focused local pilot for your vehicle type and routes, inspect the real job information available to your account, and decide whether a 7-day access pass is worth testing. The current UK individual price hypothesis is £9.99 for 7 days, with no per-job platform fee and no automatic recurring billing.

The pilot should clearly disclose the number and freshness of real listings, location/vehicle fit, poster verification status, payment terms where supplied, and any features not yet live. Do not advertise earnings, guaranteed work, live payment acceptance or verified job volume until these are evidenced.

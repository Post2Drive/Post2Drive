# Launch plan — controlled validation pilot

**Decision:** Do not run a public launch countdown yet. The current product is not cleared for unrestricted public launch and live payments/email are not verified end to end.

## Gate 0 — before inviting external users
- [ ] Select one UK geography/corridor and one vehicle segment.
- [ ] Confirm enough genuine, fresh jobs exist for that segment to make the pilot credible.
- [ ] Recruit a first cohort of repeat load posters and agree listing quality expectations.
- [ ] Complete the core browser/mobile journey checks: signup/login, market isolation, posting/search, bids, acceptance, delivery status, cancellation and account deletion.
- [ ] Confirm privacy/terms/support and eligibility/document language for the pilot.
- [ ] Keep live payments/email verification disabled until their end-to-end provider paths are verified.
- [ ] Assign support, moderation and incident owners.

## Pilot sequence (dates to be set after the gates)
1. **Supply setup:** onboard posters, validate listings and measure freshness.
2. **Closed driver cohort:** invite a small group of drivers in the same geography/vehicle segment.
3. **Observe:** track time to relevant job, first bid, poster response, acceptance and completion.
4. **Interview:** speak to active users and those who did not return.
5. **Decision:** expand only if the cohort shows repeat poster activity and drivers report sufficient relevant work; otherwise narrow the segment or change the offer.

## Success criteria to agree before the pilot
These are proposed measurement categories, not yet numeric targets:
- Share of new drivers who see at least one genuinely relevant listing during first session.
- Time from listing publication to first qualified bid.
- Poster response rate and accepted-job rate.
- Completed jobs and repeat activity over 2–4 weeks.
- Paid conversion and retention, only if the paid flow is safely enabled.
- Complaints, stale/fraudulent listings, cancellations and support load.

Set numeric thresholds after baseline interviews and a real supply inventory. Do not invent a success threshold now.

## Launch-day run sheet
Not applicable until the gates pass and a real launch date is chosen. Do not use a fake launch date or urgency.

## 30-day review
Review weekly: supply quality, relevant job availability, driver activation, poster retention, job completion, support burden, market separation and economics. Stop or narrow if drivers repeatedly see no relevant work.

# Offer design — Post2Drive

## Buyer outcome
Find suitable work with less wasted time, better route/vehicle fit and clearer job information. This is the outcome to test; the product has not yet proven that it delivers it consistently.

## Main obstacles and response
| Obstacle | Offer/product response to test | Evidence required |
|---|---|---|
| “Will there be jobs for my vehicle and route?” | Show a truthful live local supply preview and filters | Timestamped listing inventory by region and vehicle type |
| “Why pay before I know the board works?” | Let prospects inspect available listings before purchasing, where product/security design allows | Conversion test and clear access-control rules |
| “Are these jobs real?” | Poster checks, report flow and visible verification status | Documented verification process and audit trail |
| “What are the payment terms?” | Display poster-provided payment terms before a bid/acceptance | Required field coverage and user testing |
| “Will this work on my phone?” | Mobile-first search, saved filters and job alerts if implemented | Browser/device usability tests; do not claim alerts until verified |
| “What if I don't want to renew?” | Explain one-off 7-day access, expiry and no automatic renewal | Checkout and account-state tests |
| “How does it compare to incumbent boards?” | Focused local pilot with transparent supply and feedback | Actual pilot usage and comparison interviews |

## Proposed stack
- **Core:** focused access to relevant local freight/courier opportunities and the job lifecycle in one account.
- **Trust layer:** clear job details, poster/driver verification status, payment terms, reporting and proof-of-delivery workflow as supported by the product.
- **Transparency:** state the exact access period and price, no automatic recurring billing under the current model, and no per-job platform fee.
- **Pilot support:** a direct feedback channel for early drivers/posters; only promise it once staffed.

## Value equation (hypothesis, 1–10)
- Desired outcome: 8 — work discovery and less unproductive capacity matter.
- Perceived likelihood: 3 — no validated evidence of local job density or paid conversion yet.
- Time to result: 4 — depends on relevant jobs being present.
- Effort/sacrifice: 5 — onboarding and upfront payment create friction.

The biggest lever is not adding bonuses; it is increasing credible proof of local supply and trust.

## Guarantee / scarcity
Do not offer an earnings guarantee or “jobs guaranteed.” No evidence supports an affordable guarantee. Do not manufacture urgency. A limited pilot cohort is legitimate only if there is a real operational capacity limit and a stated end date.

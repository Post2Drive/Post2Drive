# Competitor map — first-pass desk research

**Date checked:** 23 October 2026. This is a public-source scan, not a complete competitor census. Public prices and claims can change; verify them directly before using in sales material.

## UK — direct and adjacent alternatives

| Competitor | Model / positioning | Public pricing evidence found | Implication for Post2Drive |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Courier Exchange](https://courierexchange.co.uk/) | Established courier load board; advertises loads and says its marketplace has around 15,000 daily load postings excluding duplicates | Official FAQ says plans vary and current prices are supplied on enquiry; it does not publish a single current price | Incumbent scale is a key challenge. A new entrant needs a specific underserved segment, trust advantage or better economics—not just another board. |
| [Same Day Courier Network](https://samedaycouriernetwork.com/for-drivers) | Driver-facing load/job board | Public page advertises £39/month + VAT and says no per-job fees | Competes on lower access cost and mobile tools. Verify live offer and contract terms directly. |
| [HaulageBoard](https://haulageboard.com/pricing) | Load board with free posting, subscription to quote and a fee when a job is agreed | Public page states £39.98/month after its promotional period and £4.98 per side when a job is agreed; the page also displays a time-limited promotion | Competes on transparency, no long contract and visible payment terms. Confirm pricing at the point of comparison. |
| [Pay A Van](https://www.payavan.com/become-a-driver) | Courier/removals marketplace, bid-based, no monthly driver subscription | Public page says free to join, no monthly fee and commission on completed jobs | Strong alternative to paying before finding work. Post2Drive must prove the subscription creates enough value to offset this alternative. |
| [Hauly](https://www.hauly.co.uk/blog/courier-work-platforms-uk-2026) | Direct-booking courier platform, described in a third-party comparison | Comparison describes no driver subscription and commission on completed jobs | Different monetisation model; validate current terms on its official site before relying on comparison figures. |
| [Shiply](https://www.shiply.com/) | Quote marketplace for delivery/removals | Commission/quote economics vary; exact current driver economics not established in this scan | Substitute for occasional jobs; relevant to drivers who prefer pay-on-win rather than access fees. |

### UK positioning hypothesis
Post2Drive's current £9.99 one-off 7-day individual access is cheaper upfront than some subscription load boards, but it is **not automatically better value** than free-to-join/commission models. No per-job fee is a differentiator only if drivers actually find enough suitable jobs.

Potential positioning to test: **transparent weekly access to relevant jobs, with clear vehicle/route fit and no per-job platform fee**. Do not claim better job volume, faster payment, higher earnings or more reliable posters without evidence.

## India — direct alternatives

| Competitor | Model / positioning | Evidence found | Implication for Post2Drive |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Vahak](https://vahak.in/) | Freight/truck-load marketplace with bidding | A recent third-party comparison describes it as an open marketplace with bidding | Direct conceptual overlap; interview transporters and drivers about route density, trust and existing network usage. |
| [BlackBuck](https://blackbuck.com/) | Trucking/logistics platform and owner-operator network | A recent third-party app comparison places it among the large Indian trucking networks | Competes with network scale and adjacent services; avoid assuming a generic load board is enough. |
| [WheelsEye](https://wheelseye.com/) | Freight matching with fleet/vehicle services | Included in recent Indian truck-load app comparisons | Competes through broader logistics functionality, not just listings. |
| [Porter](https://porter.in/) | Primarily intra-city goods transport and driver-partner model | Recent third-party comparison distinguishes it from open load boards | Substitute for some local jobs, but not identical to a bid-based freight exchange. |
| [TruckSuvidha](https://trucksuvidha.com/) | Load board listing loads and available trucks | Recent third-party comparison describes it as an established load board | Direct substitute; likely corridor-specific experience matters. |

### India positioning hypothesis
Do not copy UK weekly pricing into India. First identify a specific state/corridor, vehicle segment, poster type, existing acquisition channel and local willingness to pay. The source brief does not provide a validated India price, demand baseline or operating budget.

## The gap to test—not a proven market gap
A possible opening is a simpler, transparent marketplace for a narrowly defined corridor/vehicle segment where existing options have poor fit or trust. Evidence is not yet strong enough to say that competitors leave this gap unserved.

# Post2Drive — business plan (first-pass)

**Verdict: INCOMPLETE — not enough data to determine profitability.**

## Business
A two-sided marketplace for posting, finding, bidding on, accepting and managing delivery/freight jobs. Current model uses one-off 7-day access for UK and supported euro markets; India is separate and needs its own pricing and acquisition validation.

## Current UK access prices in the reviewed application source
- Individual: £9.99 / 7 days
- Fleet 2–5 vehicles: £16.99 / 7 days
- Fleet 6–15 vehicles: £24.99 / 7 days
- Fleet 16–30 vehicles: £36.99 / 7 days
- Fleet 31–55 vehicles: £49.99 / 7 days
- Fleet 56+ vehicles: custom

These are implemented commercial rules in source, not evidence of live checkout or willingness to pay. No automatic recurring billing and no per-job fee are described in the current model.

## Strategic verdict
**Proceed with a controlled validation pilot; do not scale or claim profitability yet.**

## Evidence-based priorities
1. Choose one UK geography/corridor and one vehicle segment.
2. Onboard genuine load posters before driver acquisition.
3. Ensure the first driver session shows relevant, fresh, credible jobs.
4. Test the current price hypothesis only after the supply picture is honest and visible.
5. Keep India as a separate research and launch plan with locally validated pricing and operations.
6. Complete launch gates for email, payment provider, browser/mobile usability, legal policies, support, monitoring and backup/restore.

## Competitive landscape
The UK has established paid load boards and free-to-join/commission alternatives; India has established freight and truck-load platforms. The defensible gap is not yet proven. Compete on a narrow, evidenced user problem—not unsupported claims about more jobs or higher earnings.

## Economics
Not calculated. Required inputs include paid users by plan, conversion, renewal/repeat purchase, refunds, payment costs, hosting/support/moderation costs, acquisition spend, poster acquisition costs and the value/retention of completed-job cohorts. Do not enter guessed zeros to force a profitability verdict.

## Validation plan
- Interview 15–20 drivers and 8–10 posters.
- Recruit posters in a single geography and validate listing quality/freshness.
- Invite a small matched driver cohort.
- Track relevant job availability, bids, responses, accepted/completed jobs and repeat usage.
- Review results and objections after 2–4 weeks.
- Expand only when the cohort repeatedly demonstrates value.

## Limitations
The competitor scan is desk research from public pages and third-party discovery sources. It does not establish market share, verified competitor performance or Post2Drive demand. No simulated buyer panel has been run; no buyer count or buy rate is claimed. This plan is a first-pass research artifact, not financial, legal or tax advice.
